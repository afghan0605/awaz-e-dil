Delete Your Awaz-e-Dil Account

Account & Data Removal Resource

Overview

At Awaz-e-Dil, we support your right to complete control over your account and personal data. You can delete your account at any time either directly within the mobile application or by submitting an external request if you cannot access the app.

Warning: Account Deletion Is Permanent

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. All data associated with your account will be immediately purged from our servers and cannot be recovered.

Option 1: How to Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

If you have access to the Awaz-e-Dil mobile app, you can delete your account directly:

  1. 1Open the Awaz-e-Dil app and ensure you are logged in.
  2. 2Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom right icon).
  3. 3Scroll down to the bottom of your profile and tap the red Delete Account button.
  4. 4Read the permanent deletion warning and type DELETE in capital letters in the confirmation box.
  5. 5Tap Delete Account to execute immediate account removal.

Your session will end, and you will be returned to the authentication screen.

Option 2: External Account Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the Awaz-e-Dil mobile app (for example, if your device was lost or uninstalled), you may request account deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: Afghan0605@gmail.com
  • Subject line must be: Account Deletion Request
  • You MUST send the email from the exact email address registered with your Awaz-e-Dil account to verify account ownership.

Upon verification of account ownership, our privacy team will manually initiate permanent account deletion within 30 days and send confirmation once completed.

What Data Is Permanently Purged?

Executing an account deletion permanently deletes all of the following data associated with your account:

  • Authentication Credentials: Email address, password credentials, and Supabase Auth UUID.
  • Profile Identity: Public Display Name, Bio, and Permanent Awaz ID.
  • Published Literary Works: All poems, Shayaris, Ghazals, Nazms, and writings published by your account.
  • Interactions: All likes / appreciations given to literary pieces.
  • Saved Writings: Your entire private collection of bookmarked writings.
  • Storage Files: Avatar image files stored in our media bucket.

Data Retention Policy

Awaz-e-Dil does NOT retain any backup or copy of your account data after deletion. Once deleted, your data cannot be restored under any circumstances.
