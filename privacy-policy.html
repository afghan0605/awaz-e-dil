1. Introduction

Welcome to Awaz-e-Dil. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the user data processed through our application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, how public and private information is treated, and how you can manage or delete your data.

2. Application & Developer Identification

This Privacy Policy applies to the Awaz-e-Dil mobile application for Android. Awaz-e-Dil is a literary social platform designed for reading, writing, and sharing original poetry and writings (Shayari, Ghazal, Nazm, and general writing).

3. Information Collected

Awaz-e-Dil collects only the minimum necessary information required to provide core application functionality:

  • Account Authentication Data: Email address and password used during registration and login. Authenticated accounts are assigned a unique internal user identifier (UUID).
  • Profile Information: Permanent numeric Awaz ID (e.g., 100000+), display name, bio text, and avatar profile picture.
  • User-Generated Content: Literary titles, text content, and category selections (Shayari, Ghazal, Nazm, Writing) created and published by you.
  • Interactions & Saved Items: Post appreciations (likes) and bookmarked writings (saved posts).
  • Avatar Storage Files: Avatar image files uploaded to our secure media bucket.
What We DO NOT Collect: Awaz-e-Dil does NOT collect location data, phone contacts, microphone audio, camera captures, financial or payment information, device advertising IDs, or third-party tracking metrics.

4. How Information Is Used

We process your data strictly to operate and maintain the application:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To display your public author profile and published literary pieces.
  • To allow you to manage, edit, or delete your published poetry.
  • To manage your private saved writings collection.
  • To facilitate author discovery via display name or permanent Awaz ID search.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Public vs. Private Information

We maintain strict separation between public and private data surface area:

  • Publicly Visible Information: Display Name, Permanent Awaz ID, Bio, Avatar image, published literary posts, and post appreciation counts. This information is intentionally visible to other users for author discovery.
  • Private Information: Your email address is strictly confidential, used solely for authentication, and is NEVER exposed on public profiles or search queries. Your Saved Writings collection is strictly private and visible only to your authenticated session.

6. User-Generated Content & Ownership

You retain full ownership of the original poetry and literary works you publish on Awaz-e-Dil. By publishing content, you grant Awaz-e-Dil a non-exclusive license to display your works to app users. You may edit or delete your published posts at any time directly within the app under My Writings.

7. Infrastructure & Service Provider Role

Awaz-e-Dil uses Supabase (Supabase Inc.) as its backend infrastructure provider for authentication, database storage, and media storage. All data transmitted between the app and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption protocol.

8. Data Security

We enforce strict security controls across our platform:

  • Database access is governed by Database Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Client applications interact only via public API publishable keys; administrative keys are never included in the app.
  • Storage objects enforce folder-level user isolation.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. Awaz-e-Dil provides a self-service in-app account deletion mechanism under your Profile screen.

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently and irreversibly purged:

  • Authentication account & credentials
  • Public profile & Awaz ID
  • All published literary posts
  • All post likes & appreciations
  • All saved writings & bookmarks
  • All avatar image files stored in storage

10. External Account Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the mobile application, you may request permanent account deletion via our web resource or by contacting us directly.

For detailed instructions, please visit our Account Deletion Web Page or email Afghan0605@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

11. Children's Privacy

Awaz-e-Dil is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us to request removal.
